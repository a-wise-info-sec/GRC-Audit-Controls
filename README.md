# Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Portfolio

## Overview

This portfolio demonstrates practical, hands-on approaches to Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC), Identity and Access Management (IAM), IT control assessments, and audit readiness.

Projects draw upon my professional experience in information security, including manual user access reviews, group access assessments, compliance activities, audit readiness, and logical and physical security controls within a regulated financial environment.

Disclaimer: All projects use **_fictional_** organizations, synthetic data, and independently developed documentation based upon my experiences. No confidential employer information, production records, or proprietary procedures are reproduced.

## Areas of Focus

- **Identity & Access Management:** User access reviews, group membership analysis, access authorization, least privilege, and separation of duties.
- **IT Governance & Compliance:** Control documentation, compliance mapping, evidence management, and audit preparation.
- **Risk Management:** Control deficiencies, risk assessment, remediation recommendations, and tracking.
- **Physical Security:** Restricted-area access, physical authorization, and protection of sensitive assets.
- **Security Frameworks:** ISO 27001, NIST SP 800-53, NIST CSF, and PCI DSS, as applicable to individual project scenarios.

## Portfolio Projects

### Project 01: Manual User Access Review & Control Assessment

**Scenario:** A precial metal storage and investment company requires an assessment of logical and physical access controls.

**Objective:** Evaluate and determine whether access permissions are appropriate for assigned responsibilities, identify potential control exceptions, and demonstrate audit-ready documentation.

**Project Scope:**
- User Access Control (UAC)
- Group Access Control (GAC)
- Least-privilege review
- Segregation-of-duties assessment
- Physical access considerations
- Control testing and audit evidence
- Risk findings and remediation recommendations

**Status:** In development.

## Methodology

Projects follow a structured approach:

1. Establish the business environment and assessment scope.
2. Identify relevant systems, personnel, access rights, and control objectives.
3. Examine synthetic evidence against defined control criteria.
4. Identify and document potential exceptions.
5. Evaluate business risk and recommend remediation.
6. Prepare review documentation and an audit evidence package.
